Let customers book a car
right from your website

Add the 1Now booking bar to any site with a single line of code — your customers search, browse and book without ever leaving your page.

Try it

Add it to your site

  1. Copy the snippet below.
  2. Paste it into your website where the booking bar should appear.
  3. Replace YOUR_COMPANY_ID with your 1Now company ID.
<div id="onenow-booking-engine"></div>
<script
  src="https://cdn.1now.ai/booking-widget/v1/widget.js"
  data-company-id="YOUR_COMPANY_ID"
  data-booking-url="https://book.1now.app"
  data-target="onenow-booking-engine"
  defer></script>

Optional: data-show-fleet="true" (show the vehicle list), data-fleet-id="123" (book one specific car), data-mode="modal", data-title.
